The Law Lab is a collaboration between political science researchers at Yale University and University of California - Berkeley working on empirical social science questions about actors in the American legal system and their identities.

{% include section.html %}

## Research

The lab is currently working on a project to digitize Martindale-Hubbell directories. We are also preparing a working paper, "The Revolving Door of Justice? Employment and Sentencing Behavior Among State Trial Court Judges." [See our projects](projects).

## Team

The lab is co-led by Dr. Hunter Rendleman (Berkeley) and Dr. Allison Harris (Yale). [Meet the team](team).
